\begin{letter}{Hiring Team\\Example Studio\\Sydney, Australia}

Three experiences would help me contribute to your team:
\begin{itemize}

\item Improving system performance while preserving understandable behaviour.
\item Writing documentation and tests that help teams maintain confidence as a product grows.
\end{itemize}
I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how these skills could support your next projects. Thank you for considering my application.
\closing{Kind regards,}\encl{Curriculum vitae}
