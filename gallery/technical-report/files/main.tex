\hero{Designing for reliability}{Technical report TR-2026-04 / Example Lab}
\section*{Executive summary}This report evaluates a document-processing service and proposes a simpler architecture with predictable failure handling. The recommendation is to make work resumable, observable, and independently testable.
\section{Context and requirements}Process documents consistently, preserve source files, and explain failures in language that a researcher can act on.
\section{Architecture}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{lXX}\toprule Stage & Responsibility & Output\\\midrule Ingest & Validate and identify input & Work item\\Process & Execute bounded transformations & Result and log\\Present & Explain status and next action & Reviewable output\\\bottomrule\end{tabularx}
\section{Evaluation}Compare throughput, failure recovery, and operational complexity. Record the workload and environment so results can be reproduced.
\section{Recommendations}\begin{enumerate}\item Make intermediate results durable.\item Keep errors close to the operation that caused them.\item Measure the experience of recovering from failure.\end{enumerate}
\section{Next steps}Assign owners and define a small pilot before committing to a full migration.
