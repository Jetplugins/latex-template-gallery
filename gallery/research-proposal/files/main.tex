\section*{Project in one paragraph}Investigate how open research tools can make computational results easier to understand and reproduce. The project combines interviews, prototype development, and comparative evaluation.
\section{Research questions}
\begin{enumerate}\item What information do readers need to reproduce a result?\item Which explanations help readers identify assumptions?\item How should tools expose uncertainty and limitations?\end{enumerate}
\section{Approach}
\textbf{Discover:} interview researchers and review existing workflows.\\
\textbf{Build:} prototype an interface connecting methods, data, and findings.\\
\textbf{Evaluate:} compare task completion and comprehension.
\section{Milestones}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{lX}\toprule Period & Deliverable\\\midrule Months 1--3 & Literature review and study design\\Months 4--6 & Interviews and first prototype\\Months 7--9 & Evaluation and analysis\\Months 10--12 & Research paper and open materials\\\bottomrule\end{tabularx}
\section{Expected contribution}A tested design framework, a documented prototype, and reusable study materials.
\section{Ethics and feasibility}Describe recruitment, consent, data handling, resource needs, and principal risks.
