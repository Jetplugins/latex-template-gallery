\section*{The question guiding my work}How can computing tools help people understand a result well enough to question it, reproduce it, and build on it? My work connects human-centred design with reproducible research practice.
\section{What I have built}I have developed tools that link questions, data, and computational methods. Working with researchers exposed a recurring gap: successful execution does not necessarily produce an understandable explanation.
\section{A research agenda}
\subsection*{1. Make assumptions visible}Study how people identify the conditions under which a result holds, and put those conditions close to the evidence.
\subsection*{2. Support meaningful reproduction}Communicate not just whether a workflow ran, but which aspects of a finding were reproduced.
\subsection*{3. Evaluate explanations with readers}Combine controlled studies with field deployments to measure comprehension and the ability to detect problems.
\section{Contribution to a research community}Share study materials, maintain reusable software, mentor students, and connect methods across disciplines. Replace these illustrative claims with specific evidence from your own work.
